COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement is entered into between Landlord and Tenant for the premises described herein.

1. Lease Term
The initial term of this Lease shall be three (3) years, commencing on the Commencement Date.

2. Automatic Renewal
This Lease shall automatically renew for successive one-year periods unless Tenant delivers written notice of intent to vacate.

3. Rent and Fees
Tenant shall pay monthly rent as set forth in Exhibit A, together with applicable taxes and common area maintenance fees.

4. Liability
Landlord shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from Tenant's use of the premises, and there shall be no limit on Tenant's liability for damage to the premises caused by Tenant's negligence.

5. Assignment
Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet the premises without the prior written consent of Landlord.

6. Default
If Tenant fails to pay rent when due, Landlord may terminate this Lease and pursue all available remedies.
