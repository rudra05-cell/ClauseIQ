VENDOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Vendor Supply Agreement is entered into between the Buyer and the Supplier.

1. Supply Term
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for two (2) years.

2. Renewal
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless Buyer provides written notice of termination at least forty-five (45) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

3. Termination for Convenience
Buyer may terminate this Agreement for convenience at any time upon fifteen (15) days written notice to Supplier.

4. Liability for Intellectual Property Infringement
Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, there shall be no limit on Supplier's liability for claims arising from infringement of a third party's intellectual property rights by the supplied goods.

5. General Liability
For all other claims, each party's liability shall not exceed the total amounts paid under this Agreement in the preceding twelve (12) months.

6. Warranty
Supplier warrants that all goods delivered will conform to the specifications set forth in Exhibit B for a period of one (1) year from delivery.
