CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement is entered into between the Client and the Consultant.

1. Services
Consultant shall provide strategic advisory services to Client as described in Exhibit A.

2. Term and Termination
This Agreement shall continue for six (6) months and may be terminated by either party for convenience upon thirty (30) days written notice to the other party.

3. Renewal
Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement may be renewed for additional six-month periods only upon mutual written agreement of both parties.

4. Limitation of Liability
Each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid to Consultant in the three (3) months preceding the claim, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

5. Non-Competition
For a period of six (6) months following termination, Consultant agrees not to provide substantially similar advisory services to Client's direct competitors within Client's primary metropolitan market.

6. Independent Contractor Status
Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee of Client.
