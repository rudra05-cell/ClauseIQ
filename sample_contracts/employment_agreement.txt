EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is entered into between the Company and the Employee.

1. Position and Duties
Employee shall serve as Senior Sales Director and shall perform such duties as assigned by the Company.

2. Term of Employment
Employment under this Agreement shall continue until terminated in accordance with Section 3.

3. Termination
The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon fourteen (14) days written notice to Employee.

4. Non-Competition
During employment and for a period of five (5) years following termination, Employee agrees not to engage in any business that competes with the Company anywhere in the world, including through employment, consulting, or ownership interest in any competing enterprise.

5. Non-Solicitation
Employee agrees not to solicit any customers or employees of the Company for a period of two (2) years following termination.

6. Confidential Information
Employee acknowledges that all confidential information obtained during employment remains the property of the Company.
